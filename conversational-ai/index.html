The project

An AI persona designed for natural, real-time conversation.

The project explores how a digital avatar can listen, understand context, retrieve relevant personal knowledge, generate a response, determine emotional state, and speak back in a natural voice—all as part of a real-time conversational pipeline.

Local STTGPU-accelerated speech recognition
RAG MemoryPersona-aware knowledge retrieval
Real-timeSocket-based avatar communication
The challenge

Conversation is more than sending text to an LLM.

A convincing conversational avatar requires several independent systems to operate as one experience. Speech must be transcribed accurately, the AI needs relevant persona context, responses must be generated with low enough latency, and the result must drive voice, emotion, and avatar behaviour consistently.

The architecture was designed to keep these systems modular while exposing a simple real-time interface to browser and Unity clients.
